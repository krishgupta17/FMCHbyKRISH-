01 · Business model

What drives the franchise?

ITC spans packaged foods, personal care, education and stationery, agri-business, paperboards and packaging, hotels, and cigarettes. For an FMCG-focused view, segment mix and capital allocation matter; group revenue is not a pure consumer-staples measure.

02 · Financial performance

Read beyond revenue.

A practical financial-analysis lens. The revenue reference is carried over from the supplied presentation; other financial values should be populated from the latest audited filings.

Scale, growth & mix

The supplied presentation lists ₹21,430 Cr for FY25 FMCG–Others. This is not total consolidated group revenue. Track volume separately from price/mix; review segment EBITDA, margins, working capital and cash conversion. The supplied dashboard does not include audited segment margin figures.

03 · Management discussion & analysis

Management’s operating agenda.

Assess brand-building, distribution expansion, premiumisation and operating leverage. Packaged foods benefit from convenience and brand trust, but demand remains sensitive to rural income, household budgets and competitive pricing.

04 · Principal risks

What could break the thesis?

Cigarettes face high excise/GST and regulatory risk; commodity inflation in wheat, edible oils, paper fibre, energy and packaging can compress margins. Foods and personal care face national brands, regional players and private labels. Portfolio complexity and demerger-related changes can complicate comparisons.
